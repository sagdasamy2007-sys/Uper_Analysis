# 🚕 Uber Trip Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end **Uber Trip Analysis Dashboard** created in **Power BI**. The dashboard analyzes thousands of trip records to extract meaningful insights regarding revenue performance, peak operational hours, trip distribution by time/vehicle, customer payment preferences, and location hotspots.

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Business Highlights

### 1️⃣ Overview Performance
- **Total Revenue & Bookings:** Processed **16.9K bookings** generating **$289.9K in revenue** with an average booking value of **$17** per trip.
- **Trip Metrics:** Average trip distance was **4 miles** (Total: 69K miles), with an average duration of **18 minutes**.
- **Day vs. Night Split:** Daytime trips dominated the demand at **64% (11K trips)** compared to **36% (6K trips)** for night trips.
- **Payment Method:** **Uber Pay (Digital Payment)** was the preferred method at **66% (11K trips)**, while **Cash** accounted for **33% (6K trips)**[span_8] .

### 2️⃣ Vehicle Analysis
- **UberX** generated the highest volume and revenue, contributing **6,336 bookings** and **$110,390.9** in total revenue.
- **Uber Black, UberXL, and Uber Comfort** showed steady performance, each contributing around **2.7K bookings** and over **$46K** in revenue.

### 3️⃣ Location & Route Insights
- **Top Hotspot:** **Lenox Hill East** was identified as both the most frequent pickup and dropoff point .
- **Longest Trip:** The farthest recorded trip spanned **144.1 miles** (Pickup: Lower East Side → Drop-off: Crown Heights North).
- High revenue areas include **JFK Airport** and **Lenox Hill East**.

### 4️⃣ Time & Demand Patterns
- Peak ride volume occurs in the afternoon and early evening hours between **1:00 PM – 6:00 PM**.
- **Sunday and Saturday** exhibited higher overall booking volumes compared to mid-week days.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools Used
- **Power BI Desktop:** Dashboard design, visual formatting, and storytelling.
- **Power Query:** Data transformation, cleaning, and custom metrics.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Calculated measures for revenue, averages, distances, and ride filters.
