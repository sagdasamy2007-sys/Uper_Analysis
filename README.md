# 🚕 Uber Trip Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end **Uber Trip Analysis Dashboard** created in **Power BI**. The dashboard analyzes thousands of trip records to extract meaningful insights regarding revenue performance, peak operational hours, trip distribution by time/vehicle, customer payment preferences, and location hotspots.

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Business Highlights

### 1️⃣ Overview Performance
- **Total Revenue & Bookings:** Processed **16.9K bookings** generating **$289.9K in revenue** with an average booking value of **$17** per trip[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
- **Trip Metrics:** Average trip distance was **4 miles** (Total: 69K miles), with an average duration of **18 minutes**[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
- **Day vs. Night Split:** Daytime trips dominated the demand at **64% (11K trips)** compared to **36% (6K trips)** for night trips[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
- **Payment Method:** **Uber Pay (Digital Payment)** was the preferred method at **66% (11K trips)**, while **Cash** accounted for **33% (6K trips)**[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).

### 2️⃣ Vehicle Analysis
- **UberX** generated the highest volume and revenue, contributing **6,336 bookings** and **$110,390.9** in total revenue[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
- **Uber Black, UberXL, and Uber Comfort** showed steady performance, each contributing around **2.7K bookings** and over **$46K** in revenue[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).

### 3️⃣ Location & Route Insights
- **Top Hotspot:** **Lenox Hill East** was identified as both the most frequent pickup and dropoff point[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
- **Longest Trip:** The farthest recorded trip spanned **144.1 miles** (Pickup: Lower East Side → Drop-off: Crown Heights North)[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).
- High revenue areas include **JFK Airport** and **Lenox Hill East**[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).

### 4️⃣ Time & Demand Patterns
- Peak ride volume occurs in the afternoon and early evening hours between **1:00 PM – 6:00 PM**[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).
- **Sunday and Saturday** exhibited higher overall booking volumes compared to mid-week days[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).
